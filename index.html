About Us

Beautiful furniture, made for how you actually live.

Interiors by Uyi is a furniture brand based in Benin City, Edo State. We source and curate beautiful, comfortable, and practical furniture for homes and personal spaces across Nigeria.

We believe your home should feel like you — not like a showroom. That's why we focus on pieces that are genuinely comfortable, well-made, and easy to live with every day. No matter where you are in Nigeria, we'll get it to your door.

Materials & Style

Good materials, comfortable designs.

We gravitate towards warm finishes, natural textures, and furniture that actually feels good to sit in, sleep on, or use every day. Nothing too precious, nothing too stiff. Just well-made pieces that suit real life in Nigerian homes.

  • Warm wood finishes — walnut, oak, and mahogany tones
  • Comfortable upholstery in linen, velvet, and boucle fabrics
  • Neutral colour palettes that work with any décor
  • Practical, everyday designs that don't sacrifice style
  • Solid construction built to handle daily use
